What does your clinic actually need?

Not every clinic needs a full system. For a clinic with one or two doctors the first problem is usually appointments: missed calls and patients who do not turn up. A website with online booking and WhatsApp reminders, starting with us from EGP 84,000, or a subscription to ready-made clinic software, is enough.

With more doctors and branches other problems appear: the patient file is split between paper and devices, treatment plans with sessions and payments that nobody follows up, revenue whose source is not known precisely, and doctors’ commissions calculated by hand. That is where a full management system comes in, scoped after a free analysis session. A centre that wants a patient portal and app is scoped after a free analysis session.

What drives the price of a clinic system?

The price moves with the number of modules, the depth of each, and how particular your speciality is:

  • The number of doctors, branches, rooms and devices that are booked.
  • Appointments: different service durations, waiting lists, recurring bookings for sessions.
  • Patient file: medical history, visits, images and attachments, prescriptions.
  • Speciality forms: the dental chart, before and after photos for dermatology, physiotherapy sessions.
  • Treatment plans, packages and instalments, tracking paid and outstanding amounts.
  • Billing and e-invoicing, and insurance, approvals and claims.
  • Doctors’ commissions and their share of revenue.
  • Stock of materials and consumables, with low-stock and expiry alerts.
  • A patient portal or app, WhatsApp reminders, and a bot that books automatically.

Ready-made subscription or a system you own?

Ready-made clinic software suits a start: quick activation, a low first cost and proven features. Ask three things before you subscribe: can you export complete patient files in a usable format if you decide to move? Where is the data hosted? And what does the subscription become when you add a doctor or a branch?

A system you own costs more at the start and suits those with their own way of working or an expansion plan: speciality forms designed for your doctors, packages and instalments as you sell them, reports as management wants them, integration with WhatsApp and accounting, and data under your control. Compare the total cost over three years at the number of doctors and branches you expect, not the first month’s price.

Health data, privacy and regulatory requirements

Patient data is among the most sensitive data there is. in Egypt it falls under the Personal Data Protection Law, and the health sector has its own laws and regulations under the Ministry of Health and the competent authorities. We do not give legal advice, and deciding what applies to your facility is your responsibility with your adviser. Our role is to build the system so it helps you comply.

In practice, ask any system for: precise permissions so a receptionist sees appointments but not diagnoses, a log of everyone who opened or changed a file, data encryption, tested backups, and the option of hosting in the region on request. If your clinic deals with insurance companies, connecting to the approved national platforms for exchanging claims, such as NPHIES, has conditions and approval from the body that runs it, and should be priced as a separate item after its requirements are studied.

Hidden costs in clinic systems

Migrating patient files is the largest: thousands of files from an old system or from paper and Excel, which need cleaning and matching before the move. Then training, because a system the doctors do not use themselves turns into an expensive appointment calendar. Allow time to train each role on its own screens only.

Then messages: the platform’s fee for a WhatsApp utility message is around four halalas, and with a confirmation and a reminder for every appointment it becomes noticeable in a busy clinic. Add yearly support of 15% to 25% of the implementation cost, e-invoicing integration if it is not part of the system, and hardware such as printers and card readers. If you want to connect medical devices or an outside laboratory, each connection is a small project priced on its own.

How to compare two clinic system quotes

Ask for a demo that follows a complete patient journey in your speciality: booking, reception, a visit recorded by the doctor, a treatment plan, an invoice and a payment, then a follow-up appointment. Then compare:

  • Are your speciality’s forms really there, or one generic form for every speciality?
  • Are treatment plans, instalments and doctors’ commissions included?
  • Are patient file migration and training inside the price?
  • How are permissions set, and is there an access log for files?
  • Where is the data hosted, and can it be exported in full at any time?
  • Are e-invoicing and insurance integration included, or separate items?
  • How fast is support during the clinic’s evening hours?

What to prepare before asking for a quote

At AETHER we start with a written scope and a contract, payments tied to each phase going live, a written delivery date that we keep, and fast technical support after go-live. The code and data belong to the clinic, and we sign an NDA on request. Prepare the following and we will send a written proposal within 24 hours:

  • Your specialities and the number of doctors and branches.
  • Your current system or method, and roughly how many patient files you have.
  • The three problems you most want solved first.
  • Whether you deal with insurance companies or take self-pay only.
  • How you sell services: per session, as a package, or as a treatment plan in instalments.
  • Whether you need a patient portal or app in phase one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a clinic management system cost in Egypt?

With us a custom clinic system starts from EGP 282,000: a custom appointment system for several doctors and branches usually costs EGP 282,000–562,500, while a full system with patient files and billing and centres with a patient portal and app are scoped after a free analysis session. A clinic website with online booking starts from EGP 84,000. Ready-made software is sold on a monthly subscription that varies by provider.

How long does it take to implement a clinic system?

A website with online booking takes 4 to 6 weeks, a custom appointment system 6 to 12 weeks, and a full system 3 to 4 months. You can go live in phases: appointments and reception first, then the patient file and billing, then stock and reports. Migrating old files is what most needs early planning.

I have a small clinic. Do I need a custom system?

Usually not. A clinic with one or two doctors is well served by ready-made clinic software on subscription, or a website with online booking and WhatsApp reminders. A custom system earns its cost when branches and specialities multiply, when you have your own way of handling treatment plans and packages, or when yearly subscriptions approach the cost of a system you own.

Is patient data secure?

The system must be designed for it: permissions for each role, a log of every view and change, encryption, tested backups, and the option of hosting in the region on request. Patient data falls under the Personal Data Protection Law and health sector regulations. Ask for these items in writing in the quote; the data and the code belong to the clinic.

Does the system connect to insurance companies?

It can, but as a separate item. Exchanging approvals and claims with insurers goes through approved national platforms that have conditions and approval from the body that runs them, and the integration requirements need studying before pricing. If most of your revenue comes from insurance, say so from the start, because it changes the whole billing design.

Does the system support dental, dermatology and cosmetic clinics?

Yes, and each speciality has its own forms. Dentistry needs a dental chart and multi-session treatment plans. Dermatology and cosmetic clinics need before and after photos, session packages and device tracking. Physiotherapy needs session programmes and progress assessment. Make sure the quote names your speciality’s forms, not a generic patient file.

Can patients book and pay from their phones?

Yes. A patient can book from the website, the app or a WhatsApp conversation, pay a deposit or the consultation fee through mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay, Tabby and Tamara, and receive a confirmation and a reminder. Card details are processed by the licensed payment gateway and are not stored in the clinic’s system. Prepayment is one of the most effective ways to cut no-shows.

Can I move patient files from my current system?

In most cases yes, if the current system allows data export. Patient data, visits and balances are moved after cleaning and matching, and attachments and images are uploaded if they can be extracted. Ask your current provider for an export file early, because the shape of that file decides the migration effort and cost.

Does the system issue invoices that comply with e-invoicing?

The system must issue invoices that meet the requirements of the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA), and phase two requires a connection to the Fatoora platform according to your wave. In a custom system the integration is built into billing; published market offers put connecting an existing system at EGP 142,500–270,000. Ask your accountant about the tax treatment of health services.
