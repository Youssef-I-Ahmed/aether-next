What drives the price of an AI agent?

An AI agent, or digital employee, is software that understands what a customer says and then does something about it: answers, books, sends a quotation, opens a ticket, hands over to a person. Unlike a menu bot, it does not wait for the customer to press a number. And what separates one agent from another is how many tasks it really carries out inside your systems. That is why it is priced by what it can do, not by message count.

This guide goes beyond our WhatsApp bot guide and covers what sits on top of it: voice calls, and running several channels on one body of knowledge. These are the items that move the number:

  • Channels: WhatsApp only, WhatsApp and the website, or the phone as well.
  • Tasks: answers only, or booking, quotations, tracking, collections and tickets.
  • Integrations: CRM, ERP, booking system, online store or phone system.
  • Knowledge source: static files, or a live read of prices, stock and appointments from your system.
  • Languages and dialects, and understanding voice notes and calls.
  • Handover to a human with a summary of the conversation and its context.
  • Monthly volume of conversations and calls, and peak hours.
  • Permissions and privacy: what the agent may say, do and see.

First version versus a full digital employee

A successful first version is small: one channel, the twenty questions you hear most, one task that eats your team’s time such as booking an appointment or logging a quotation request, and a clear handover to a person when needed. That sits in the basic agent range of EGP 37,500–120,000, launches within a few weeks, and gives you real conversations to build on.

A full digital employee works across several channels on one body of knowledge: the customer starts on the website and continues on WhatsApp, then phones and the voice agent already knows their earlier request. It acts inside your systems too: reads available slots, records the customer in the CRM, sends the invoice or payment link. With that integration the project moves into the integrated automation range of EGP 120,000–300,000 for setup, with voice as an added item.

What we do not recommend postponing: building the knowledge and the tasks once, in one place. If the WhatsApp agent is built separately from the website agent, you pay twice and get two different answers to the same question.

Voice agents: what is different from WhatsApp?

In a text chat the customer tolerates a few seconds of waiting. On a call they do not. A voice agent goes through three steps for every sentence: it turns speech into text, understands and decides, then turns the reply into speech. The experience depends on how fast that loop runs, on understanding dialect over a phone line that may be poor, and on the agent stopping when the caller interrupts.

The cost is different as well: it is billed per minute rather than per message, plus the phone number, the link to the company phone system if there is one, and transfer to a person for the cases it cannot settle. We quote no per-minute figure because we found no reliable published range in the local market, and provider prices change. Ask for a written estimate at 500 calls a month and at 5,000.

The best uses to start with: answering out of hours, confirming appointments, handling repeated questions, and taking the caller’s details and request before sending a summary to the person responsible. Negotiations and complaints stay with a human.

Running costs: what you pay every month

An AI agent is a service that runs every day, not software you buy once. Market estimates put the running cost of integrated automation at EGP 45,000–120,000 a month, covering hosting, language model usage, monitoring and improving the answers. Ask in every quote: what exactly is inside the monthly figure, and what is billed on top?

Channel fees come on top. The official WhatsApp Business platform charges about EGP 2.85 for a marketing message and about EGP 0.6 for a utility message started by the business, according to prices published by service providers, and these may change. Calls are billed per minute. Ask for the WhatsApp, phone and language model accounts to be registered in your organisation’s name, so you see the real invoices and can change provider.

A ready-made platform or an agent built for your company?

Ready-made subscription platforms, some of which start in the market at EGP 480–8,235 a month for WhatsApp replies, organise the inbox, send campaigns and reply with menus. Some add an AI auto-reply based on files you upload. That is enough for a business with a limited set of questions and no need to act inside its own systems.

An agent built for your company knows your business: it reads prices and appointments from your system, carries the task through to the end, and stays within limits that you set. It costs more at the start, but it does work that used to need an employee, and you own its code, data and conversations. A working rule: if the job is only to reply, start ready-made. If the job is to complete the transaction, a custom agent is the route.

Common mistakes that raise the cost or sink the project

The first is launching the agent on everything from day one. An agent attempting twenty tasks masters few of them, and the customer judges it on the first wrong answer. The second is leaving it without limits: an agent that promises a discount or a delivery date that does not exist costs you more than it saves. Write down what it must not say, and when it hands over to a person.

The third is neglecting review after launch. The conversations handed to a person in the first month are your real improvement list. The fourth concerns data: customer conversations and call recordings are personal data under the Personal Data Protection Law, and recording calls has its own requirements. Decide with your adviser what is recorded, where it is kept and for how long, and tell the caller. We do not give legal advice; we build the system around what you decide.

How to compare two quotes, and what to prepare

Ask for a live test on real questions from your customers, written and spoken and in dialect, and ask the agent something it cannot answer to see how it behaves. At AETHER we commit to a written delivery date, with fast technical support after launch. The code, accounts and conversations are yours, and we send a written proposal within 24 hours.

  • Which tasks does the agent really carry out in your systems, as opposed to talk about?
  • Do all channels share one body of knowledge, or is there a separate agent per channel?
  • What does the monthly figure cover, and who pays for messages, minutes and the language model?
  • How does it hand a conversation or call to a person, and what does that person see?
  • Where are conversations and recordings kept, and can they be exported and deleted?
  • Prepare before you ask: your most frequent questions with approved answers, the tasks you need, the systems it will connect to, monthly conversation and call volume, and the cases that go to a person.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI digital employee cost for my company?

A basic agent that answers customers on one channel costs EGP 37,500–120,000 in the market, and an agent connected to your systems EGP 120,000–300,000 for setup, with running costs of EGP 45,000–120,000 a month. With us an AI agent starts from EGP 37,500; the real figure is set once the channels, tasks and integrations are known.

How much does a voice agent that answers calls cost?

There is no reliable published range for it in the local market, so we quote no figure. The cost has two parts: setup, which depends on the number of tasks and the link to your phone system and other systems, and running costs billed by call minutes. Ask for a quote that separates the two, and a written running estimate at a set number of calls, so you can compare with your cost of human answering.

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers. An agent acts. The first tells the customer your opening hours and prices; the second checks available slots, books, sends the confirmation and records the customer in your system. The price gap between them is the cost of integration, testing and limits, which is what makes the agent do an employee’s job rather than the job of an FAQ page.

How long does it take to build the agent?

A basic agent on one channel usually takes 3 to 5 weeks, and an agent connected to your systems 6 to 10 weeks, longer with more integrations and voice. Add the time to verify the WhatsApp Business account, so start that early. What delays launch most is preparing approved answers and up-to-date prices on your side.

Does the agent understand Saudi dialect in text and voice?

In text, current models understand Standard Arabic and Gulf dialects well. In voice, accuracy is affected by line quality, noise and speaking speed, so we test on real recordings and messages from your customers before you rely on it. In any quote, ask for a trial on a sample of your own conversations rather than a prepared demo.

Can the agent work on WhatsApp, the website and the phone together?

Yes, if it is built on shared knowledge and tasks. The customer starts on the website and continues on WhatsApp, then phones and the agent finds the earlier request. This costs less than three separate agents and answers more consistently. We recommend starting with the channel most of your customers use, then adding the next once the first is stable.

What are the monthly costs after launch?

Integrated automation costs EGP 45,000–120,000 a month to run in the market, covering hosting, the language model and monitoring. On top come WhatsApp fees for messages started by the business, about EGP 2.85 for marketing and EGP 0.6 for utility messages, and call minutes for a voice agent. Ask for a written breakdown of what the monthly figure includes.

Does the agent replace customer service staff?

It replaces a large share of the repetitive work and answers around the clock, but it does not remove the need for a person on complaints, special cases and large deals. The best results come from an agent that completes simple transactions and passes the rest to a person with a clear summary. That frees your team for what needs human judgement.

Do I own the code and the conversations?

Yes. At AETHER the code, accounts and data belong to the client, and this is written into the contract before work starts. The WhatsApp Business account, the phone number, the language model and the hosting are registered in your organisation’s name, and you can export conversations at any time. We commit to a written delivery date, with fast technical support after launch.
