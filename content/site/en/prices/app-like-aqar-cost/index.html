What exactly are you building: a site for your properties or a platform for the market?

The Aqar app is a well-known platform in Egypt for properties for sale and rent, where the user searches on the map and contacts the advertiser. The name is used here for illustration only; we have no connection with the company that owns the app. Before asking about cost you need to decide which of two projects you want, because the gap between them is wide.

The first is a website or app that shows your own properties: a developer with projects, or an agency with a portfolio. That is a smaller project that starts with us from EGP 84,000 and usually costs EGP 84,000–168,750; advanced search and booking are scoped after a free analysis session. The second is an open platform where many agencies, brokers and owners advertise. That is a platform with accounts, permissions, moderation and a revenue model, starting with us from EGP 282,000 for the web, while advanced platforms are scoped after a free analysis session.

What drives the price of a real estate platform?

A property is a product with a lot of data and a location, which is what makes search the heart of the platform and its most expensive part:

  • Map search: properties shown as points, searching by drawing an area, clustering when zoomed out.
  • Property fields: type, area, rooms, district, building age, frontage, and whatever each type needs.
  • Account types: owner, broker, agency with several staff, developer with projects and units.
  • Photos, video, floor plans and virtual tours.
  • Saved searches and alerts when a matching property is listed.
  • Contact: call, WhatsApp, in-platform chat, a viewing request with a time.
  • Licence details: fields for the ad licence number and advertiser details, shown on the listing.
  • Revenue model: agency subscriptions, featured listings, developer packages.
  • Arabic and English, if your audience includes investors from abroad.

First version versus full platform

A practical first version: adding a property with its photos, fields and location; search and filters by city, district, type and price; a map; an advertiser page; contact by call and WhatsApp; and an admin panel that reviews listings. On the web this starts with us from EGP 282,000 and usually costs EGP 282,000–562,500. One city or one type of property, such as furnished apartments, land or commercial property, is enough for a strong start.

The second phase adds what usage proves is wanted: mobile apps, alerts, chat, agency accounts with several staff, and featured listings. The third phase enters the advanced range: viewing bookings with a time slot, deposit payments, virtual tours, CRM integration for agencies, and price reports by district.

Real estate regulation and ad licensing

Real estate advertising and brokerage in Egypt are regulated by the Real Estate General Authority, including requirements for licensing property ads, advertisers and platforms. These requirements change, and we do not give legal advice. Check with the Authority or a qualified adviser to learn what applies to your business before you fix the business model.

Technically, what matters is that the platform is designed from the start to accommodate these requirements: mandatory fields for licence details, showing them on the listing page, blocking incomplete listings, and a log of who published what and when. If an integration with the regulator’s services is required or available, it goes into the scope of work from the start, because adding it after launch touches the whole publishing logic.

Hidden costs and running costs

Maps are a major item: every time the map opens and every address search is billed by usage, and market estimates put maps at EGP 225,000–600,000 depending on volume. That can be reduced with smart design that caches results and loads the map only when needed. Photos and video raise storage and hosting costs as the number of properties grows.

Add the cost of content: a property platform without fresh listings has no value. Many platforms start by agreeing with specific agencies and entering their listings, and by removing expired listings regularly, because a sold property that is still published costs the visitor’s trust. Set aside 15% to 20% of the build cost a year for maintenance, and a budget for search engine optimisation, because Google searches for a district and a property type are a core source of traffic.

How to compare two real estate platform quotes

Ask for a test version and try the map search from your phone on an ordinary network, then compare:

  • Is map search real, with clustering and filters, or just a map on the property page?
  • Are the account types you need supported, such as an agency with several staff?
  • Are licence fields and the listing review process included?
  • Who pays the map fees, and what is the monthly estimate at a given number of visits?
  • Are property and district pages optimised for search engines and rendered on the server?
  • Are the code and data yours, and can listings be imported from a file or from the agency’s system?
  • How fast is technical support after launch?

Frequently asked questions

How much does an app like Aqar cost?

With us a real estate platform starts from EGP 282,000: a custom-built listings website with search and a map usually costs EGP 282,000–562,500, while a platform on web, iOS and Android with agency and broker accounts and advanced platforms with bookings, payments and integrations are scoped after a free analysis session. The real price is set once the feature list and account types are written down.

How long does it take to build a real estate platform?

An agency or developer website showing its own properties takes 3 to 8 weeks. The first version of an open platform takes 3 to 5 months, and longer with apps, bookings and payments. Map search is the most time-consuming part, and defining the fields for each property type early saves a great deal of rework.

I run a real estate agency. Do I need a platform, or is a website enough?

If your goal is to show your properties and receive enquiries, a professional website with a property section and simple search is enough, and it starts with us from EGP 84,000, usually costing EGP 84,000–168,750. An open platform is a different project, because it needs advertisers, visitors and moderation. Many agencies combine their own website with advertising on the large platforms.

Do I need a licence for a property listings platform?

Real estate advertising and brokerage in Egypt are regulated by the Real Estate General Authority, with requirements for licensing ads, advertisers and platforms. We are a software company and do not give legal advice; check with the Authority or a qualified adviser. Our role is to build the platform so it can comply: licence fields, showing them on listings, and listing review.

What is the difference between a map on the property page and map search?

The first shows where one property is, and it is simple and cheap. The second shows hundreds of properties as points that cluster and separate as you zoom, and it updates with every move of the map and every filter. It is the experience property hunters now expect. The cost gap between the two is large, so check which one the quote means.

Can the platform be connected to the agency’s CRM?

Yes. Every enquiry about a property can enter the CRM as a lead with the property name and the broker responsible, with follow-up and viewing appointments. That integration raises the platform’s value for agencies and makes them willing to pay a subscription. It can connect to an existing system, or a simple follow-up module can be built inside the platform itself.

How do I keep listings fresh?

With technology and operations together: listings that expire automatically after a period with a reminder to renew, an easy button to mark a property as sold or rented, visitor reports for expired listings, and a regular review of inactive accounts. An expired listing that stays published damages the platform’s reputation more than having few listings.

Does the app support viewing bookings and deposit payments?

Yes, and they can be added in a later phase. Viewing bookings need a calendar for the broker, with confirmation and reminders. Deposit payments connect to a licensed payment gateway that handles card details, with a clear refund policy. These features move the project into the advanced range, and payments may carry regulatory requirements to review with a specialist.

Do I own the code and the data?

Yes. At AETHER the code, accounts, servers and data belong to the client, and this is written into the contract before work starts. Store, maps and hosting accounts are registered in your company’s name, and you can export properties and enquiries at any time. We commit to a written delivery date, with fast technical support after launch.
