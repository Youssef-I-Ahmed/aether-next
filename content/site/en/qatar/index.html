Software built for the Qatari market

Qatar has one of the fastest-growing digital economies in the region, with major investment in technology infrastructure under Qatar National Vision 2030. AETHER delivers advanced software that helps Qatari companies build a strong digital presence that keeps pace with a fast-moving market.

What a software company builds for businesses in Qatar

Business in Qatar is concentrated in Doha, Lusail and Al Rayyan, and most requests are for systems that organise work already under way. Contracting and real estate companies need platforms to list units and track projects and payment certificates, institutions need service portals and internal systems built to high security standards, and retailers need a shopping experience in Arabic and English for a market with residents of many nationalities.

We connect stores and apps to local payment gateways, debit and credit cards and Apple Pay, and to the delivery companies operating in the country. We also build practical AI: a digital employee that answers enquiries and books appointments, and reports that summarise sales and operations data.

How we work with a client in Doha

We meet first in a remote discovery session and ask how the work runs today, who will use the system and what decisions you expect it to support. No more than 24 hours later you have a written proposal that sets out scope, phases, schedule and price.

The project is paid in stages, and each payment matches a delivery named in the proposal. We send a weekly progress report and hold a review meeting at each phase, in Arabic or English depending on who attends from your team. From the start, the code, the domain and the accounts are registered in your organisation’s name.

How to choose a software company in Qatar

Ask every company you are comparing for three things: published work you can open and browse on your own phone, a written scope document that says what the price includes and what it does not, and an explicit clause that the code and domain belong to you. Make sure the delivery date is written in the contract, not in a passing message.

A trial tells you more than a presentation. If you want to see live work of ours, open the /try page on our site and talk to the digital employee in writing or by voice.

FAQ

Common questions about our services in Qatar

What does it cost to hire a software company in Qatar?+

Scope decides it: the kind of project, the number of pages or screens, the features and the systems it connects to. After a discovery session we send a written quote within 24 hours, and for a first idea the price guides on our site show market ranges in Saudi riyals.

What do you offer a business looking for an app development company in Qatar?+

We design and build iOS and Android apps for bookings, orders and customer service, with an admin panel in Arabic and English and local payment-gateway integration. We work with clients in Qatar remotely and deliver in phases you try on your phone.

How do I compare software companies in Qatar?+

Compare the written proposals, not the prices alone: what the scope covers, when delivery is due, who owns the code and domain, and what technical support follows launch. The cheapest quote may leave out things you end up paying for later.

Do you work remotely with companies in Doha?+

Yes. We work with clients in Qatar remotely, with regular meetings and direct communication in Arabic or English.

Do you build bilingual websites and apps?+

Absolutely. We build digital products in Arabic and English with full right-to-left and left-to-right support and responsive design.

Do you support Qatari payment gateways?+

We integrate local and international gateways, including credit cards and Apple Pay, so payments in your store or app are simple.

Do you build solutions for government bodies and large enterprises?+

Yes. We build platforms and enterprise systems with high security standards that meet the requirements of Qatar’s public and private sectors.
