Software built for the Saudi market

Saudi Arabia is going through the largest digital transformation in its history, driven by Vision 2030. As the digital economy grows and companies expand, they need a technology partner who understands the local market and can keep pace with its ambition. AETHER delivers end-to-end websites, apps and enterprise systems built to high standards of quality and security.

What a software company builds for Saudi businesses

Most of what Saudi companies ask for falls into five kinds: a professional company website in Arabic and English, an online store connected to local payment options such as mada, Tabby and Tamara, a mobile app for customers or drivers, an internal system that runs sales, stock and staff (ERP or CRM), and an AI digital employee that answers customers on WhatsApp and the phone.

We build all of them with one team, so you do not coordinate a designer, a developer and a hosting company. The source code, the domain and the accounts are in your organisation’s name from day one.

What software projects cost in Saudi Arabia

Cost depends on the kind and size of the project, and our starting prices are public: a company website starts from EGP 5,600, an online store from EGP 5,600, a mobile app on iOS and Android from EGP 12,000, and a custom system such as an ERP or CRM from EGP 282,000.

The final price depends on scope: the pages or screens, the features and the systems to integrate. We send a written proposal with scope, phases, timeline and a price in riyals within 24 hours, and the price guides on our site cover each kind in detail.

What every system running in Saudi Arabia must comply with

A software company serving the Saudi market has to know the local requirements before it writes the first line, because adding them after launch costs more and delays go-live. These are the points we ask about in the first meeting and write into the scope:

  • E-invoicing: systems that issue invoices connected to ZATCA’s Fatoora platform to Phase 2 requirements, with the invoice data required for the applicable market.
  • Local payments: mada, Apple Pay and STC Pay, and instalments through Tabby and Tamara in stores and apps.
  • Identity and address: Nafath sign-in where needed, and the National Address in orders and shipping.
  • HR: Wage Protection files for Mudad, GOSI contributions and contract data in Qiwa.
  • Language and dates: a right-to-left Arabic interface with English where needed, and Hijri and Gregorian dates on documents.
  • Data: compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), and in-region data hosting on request.

How we work with companies in Saudi Arabia

We serve companies in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf remotely and work Saudi hours. We start with an online meeting to understand how your business runs, then a written proposal within 24 hours with scope, phases, timeline and a price in riyals, then a contract that sets the delivery date under our on-time delivery guarantee.

During the build you follow weekly progress in the client portal, your project has a WhatsApp group for daily follow-up, and every two weeks you try a working version on a real link. Payments are tied to deliveries you test yourself, and after launch you get fast technical support.

How to choose a software company in Saudi Arabia

Do not rely on "top 10" lists: most were written by a company that ranks itself first. Open the company’s published work on your phone, ask for a written proposal listing pages and features, ask whose name the code and domain will be in, and ask for the delivery date and what happens if it slips.

If the service can be tried, ask to try it before signing. Our site has a live digital employee you can write to or call, and you see what it does as it happens.

FAQ

Common questions about our services in Saudi Arabia

What does a website or an app cost in Saudi Arabia?+

With us a company website starts from EGP 5,600, an online store from EGP 5,600, a mobile app from EGP 180,000 and a custom system from EGP 282,000. The final price depends on the pages, features and integrations, and we send a written quote within 24 hours.

Are your systems compatible with ZATCA e-invoicing?+

Yes. We integrate systems and stores that issue invoices with ZATCA’s Fatoora platform to Phase 2 requirements, and our e-invoicing integration guide explains the steps and the cost.

What is the difference between a software company and a freelancer?+

A freelancer suits a small, defined task. A software company gives you a full team (analysis, design, development, testing) with a written contract and scope, a delivery date and support after launch, which is what systems your daily operations depend on need.

Do I own the code, the domain and the accounts after delivery?+

Yes. The source code, the database, the domain and the hosting and store accounts are in your organisation’s name from day one, and the contract says so. You can move the project to any team whenever you like, and nothing stays with us that could hold you back.

How do I compare two quotes from two software companies?+

Compare the scope before the price: the pages or screens and features listed item by item, integrations such as payment and e-invoicing, the timeline and payment stages, who owns the code and accounts, and what support after launch covers. The cheapest quote without a written scope usually becomes the most expensive at the first change request.

Do you provide software development services in every Saudi city?+

Yes. We work with clients across Saudi Arabia remotely, with direct communication in Arabic or English.

Do you support Saudi payment methods such as mada and Apple Pay?+

Absolutely. We integrate local and international payment options, including mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay, Stripe, Tabby and Tamara, into stores and apps.

How long does it take to build a website or an app?+

It depends on the size of the project. Company websites usually take 2–4 weeks, while apps and full platforms take 6 weeks or more. You get a clear timeline before we start.

Are your systems ready for government digital-transformation requirements?+

Yes. We build solutions that follow security and data-protection standards and meet the requirements of the Kingdom’s regulators, including ZATCA e-invoicing where it applies.
