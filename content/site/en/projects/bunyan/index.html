Bunyan

A real estate operations dashboard for managing developer partnerships, projects, and property units.

Start a similar project View dashboard
Solution type

The goal

Real estate operators needed a way to connect a developer to its projects and units, and work with that information from one place.

How it works

The dashboard groups developer records and the projects and units related to them so an operator can follow the information at the right level.

What we built

  • Developer and partnership management
  • Project and property unit organization
  • An operational dashboard for staff
