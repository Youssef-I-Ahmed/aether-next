1. Introduction

AETHER ("we", "our", or "us") operates this website and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services, including our integrations with third-party platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you connect a social media account (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook), we collect your public profile information including display name, profile picture, and follower count.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our platform, including pages visited and features used.
  • Content Data: Content you create, schedule, or publish through our platform.
  • Analytics Data: Performance metrics from connected social media accounts (likes, views, reach, impressions).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our marketing management services
  • Display analytics and performance data in your dashboard
  • Schedule and publish content to connected social media accounts
  • Send notifications and updates related to your account
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Third-Party Platform Integrations

Our service integrates with third-party platforms including TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook. When you connect these accounts, we access data through their official APIs in accordance with their respective terms of service and privacy policies. We only request permissions necessary to provide our services.

  • TikTok: We access profile information, video statistics, and publishing capabilities through the TikTok API. Data is used solely for displaying analytics and scheduling content within our platform.
  • Meta (Instagram/Facebook): We access page insights and publishing capabilities through the Meta Graph API.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security policies. Access tokens for connected social media accounts are encrypted and stored securely. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • With service providers who assist in operating our platform (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • With your explicit consent

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Connected social media account tokens can be revoked at any time through your dashboard settings.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Disconnect any connected social media accounts at any time
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

9. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

We use essential cookies and browser storage to maintain your session and preferences.

To understand how our website is used and to measure our advertising, we record visits to our public website: the pages you view, the buttons and links you click, how far you scroll, the time spent on a page, the steps of our contact forms, your device type, browser and operating system, your IP address and the approximate location (country and city) derived from it, and the campaign or search keyword that brought you to us. A random identifier is stored in your browser to connect visits from the same device. We do not sell this data and use it only to improve our website and services and to follow up on requests you send us.

We use the Google Ads tag to measure the results of our ads (including enhanced conversions, which send a hashed form of the email or phone number you submit), and Meta Pixel where enabled. When you type a phone number or email address into our quote form, we save what you have entered so far so that our team can follow up on your request even if you do not finish the form. Visit data is kept for up to 12 months. You can ask us to delete it at any time using the contact details below.
