Software built for the Emirati market

The United Arab Emirates is the region’s capital of innovation and a global hub for business and technology. With digital transformation accelerating and AI being adopted nationwide, AETHER delivers world-class software that helps companies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi stand out and grow in a competitive market.

What a software company builds for businesses in Dubai and Abu Dhabi

The UAE market is multinational and multilingual, so most of what we build there starts as a site or platform in Arabic and English together. Real estate and hospitality companies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi ask for listing, booking and lead-tracking platforms, retailers in Sharjah and Ajman ask for multi-currency stores connected to international shipping, and financial-services firms ask for secure dashboards and apps for their customers.

For payments we connect stores and apps to credit cards, Apple Pay and instalment services such as Tabby and Tamara, through gateways such as Stripe and PayTabs. Where it helps, we add an AI digital employee that answers customer questions in Arabic and English on WhatsApp.

How a website or app project runs for a client in Abu Dhabi or Dubai

We start with a discovery session over video to understand your business and what the site or app has to do, then send a written proposal within 24 hours covering scope, phases, timeline and price. Nothing is built until you have agreed to that proposal line by line.

Payments are staged, and each one is tied to a delivery you can see and try. Every week you get a short summary of what was done and what comes next, in Arabic or English to suit your team. The source code, the domain, and the store and hosting accounts are registered in your company’s name.

How to choose a software company in the UAE

There are many agencies in the UAE and their pitches look alike, so judge by what you can check. Open sites and apps the company has published on your phone and test their speed and their Arabic version, ask for a written scope that lists pages and features, confirm the code and domain will be in your name, and make sure the delivery date is written into the contract.

Try before you sign whenever you can. The /try page on our site has a live digital employee you can write to or talk to, so you see the standard of what we deliver before any commitment.

FAQ

Common questions about our services in the UAE

What should I ask an app development company in Dubai before signing?+

Ask for apps live in the stores that you can download, who will own the App Store and Google Play accounts, who owns the source code, and whether the delivery date is in the contract. With us the store accounts are opened in your company’s name and the code is yours.

What does web development cost in Abu Dhabi and Dubai?+

It depends on scope: the number of pages and languages, the features and the systems to integrate. We send a written quote within 24 hours of the discovery session, and the price guides on our site give market ranges in Saudi riyals as a first reference.

Does a software company in the UAE need to be near my office?+

Not necessarily. What protects your project is a contract with a written scope and delivery date, payments tied to deliveries, a weekly update, and ownership of the code and accounts. We work with clients in the UAE remotely on exactly those terms, in Arabic and English.

Do you develop websites for companies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?+

Yes. We build company websites, online stores and platforms in Arabic and English for clients in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, with a custom design and an admin panel you edit yourself.

Do you build mobile apps for companies in Dubai?+

Yes. We build iOS and Android apps for bookings, orders and customer service, connected to your systems and the payment gateways used in the UAE.

How do I choose a web development agency in the UAE?+

Compare quotes line by line: scope, phases, delivery date, code ownership and support after launch. A quote that does not spell these out clearly deserves attention.

Do you work remotely with companies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?+

Yes. We serve clients in every emirate remotely, with regular meetings and direct communication in Arabic and English.

Do you build multilingual websites and apps?+

Absolutely. We build digital products in Arabic, English and other languages, with full right-to-left and left-to-right support.

Do you support UAE payment gateways?+

We integrate local and international gateways such as Apple Pay, Stripe, Checkout.com and PayTabs into stores and apps.

Do you offer AI solutions for businesses?+

Yes, from chatbots and automation to data analytics and custom intelligent systems built around your business.
