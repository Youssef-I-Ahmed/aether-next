What should a medical website achieve?

Nobody browses a clinic’s website for fun. A patient arrives with questions: do they treat my condition, who is the doctor, when is the next appointment, and do they accept my insurance? A site that answers those four without a phone call takes pressure off reception and turns a visit into a booking.

So we build the site around two clear paths: "I know which doctor I want" and "I know the specialty but not the doctor". Both end at the same booking button. The phone number, WhatsApp and branches stay visible on every page, because many patients, older ones especially, still prefer to call.

The pages and content a clinic website needs

Medical content is the responsibility of your medical management. We draft, structure and build, and your doctors review and approve everything published about conditions and procedures. We write in general, informational terms, avoid promises of results and comparative claims, and add a notice that the information does not replace seeing a doctor.

  • Doctors: one page each, with approved details and clinic days
  • Specialties and services: one page per department
  • Booking: a form or a calendar, with a confirmation message
  • Branches: address, map and opening hours
  • Patient information: insurance, visit instructions and FAQs
  • Health articles written or reviewed by your doctors, if you want them

Template or custom site, and do you need live booking?

A clinic with one or two doctors needs no more than a simple site: an introduction, services, a WhatsApp button and an appointment request form that reception answers. A good template does that job at low cost, and we recommend it when it is enough.

A custom site shows its value as doctors and branches multiply: a searchable doctor directory, clinic schedules that change, and direct booking from a calendar. Live booking is an operational decision before it is a technical one. If you run a clinic system that manages appointments, we connect the site to it where the system offers an integration; otherwise we start with a request that reception confirms, so nobody is double-booked.

What gets a medical website found on Google?

Most medical searches are local and specific: a specialty plus a district or city, or a doctor’s name. That makes doctor, specialty and branch pages the most important pages on the site, and each one gets a title, a description and structured data telling Google about the facility, its doctors, its addresses and its hours.

Search engines are strict about health content and favour pages that show who wrote or reviewed them and when they were updated, so articles carry the reviewing doctor’s name and a revision date. That is what we build. Ranking depends on competition in your area and on time, and we do not promise a position.

Privacy and healthcare advertising

An appointment request carries the patient’s name and number, and sometimes the reason for the visit, which is personal and health data. We build the forms with the Personal Data Protection Law in mind: few fields, clear consent, an encrypted connection, defined access, and no health details in exposed notifications. Data can be hosted in-region on request.

Advertising healthcare services, and what may be published in offers and images, is subject to rules set by the health sector regulator. Your facility confirms the current requirements with the competent authority and its legal adviser, and we implement what you decide: the licence details you want displayed, the wording of notices, and content review before publishing.

What to prepare, and which mistakes to avoid

Take the information from its official source inside the facility, because an error in a doctor’s qualification or clinic hours costs a patient’s trust. And decide who approves medical content before we start writing.

  • The doctor list with approved details and recent photos on a consistent background
  • Specialties, services and accepted insurers
  • Branches and hours, and how booking works today
  • Common mistake: stock photos of doctors who are not yours
  • Common mistake: an old schedule, or a page for a doctor who has left
  • Common mistake: a booking form that asks for ten fields before it shows a slot

Frequently asked questions

How much does a clinic website cost?

A clinic website with us starts from EGP 84,000. The final price depends on the number of doctors and branches, the languages, how booking works and any payment or WhatsApp connection, and we send it in a written quote within 24 hours.

Can patients book directly from the website?

Yes, in one of two ways. The first is a request that reaches reception and is confirmed there, which is simplest and suits small clinics. The second is direct booking from the doctor’s calendar with confirmation and reminder. We choose with you what fits your operation, and connect to your current clinic system where it offers an integration.

Can you connect the site to the clinic system we already use?

It depends on the system. If it exposes an API for appointments, we connect booking to it so the slot appears in the doctor’s schedule straight away. If it does not, the request goes to reception to enter by hand. We check this before quoting and do not promise an integration we have not confirmed is possible.

Who writes the medical content?

We draft the informational text for specialties and services from what you give us, and your doctors review and approve it before it is published. We do not write diagnoses or treatment advice and we do not promise outcomes. Health articles are published under the name of the doctor who wrote or reviewed them, with a revision date.

Is patient data on the website safe?

We apply what the Personal Data Protection Law calls for: forms that ask only for what is needed, clear consent, an encrypted connection, roles that limit who sees requests, and a published privacy policy. Your detailed obligations as a healthcare facility are for your legal adviser to review, and we implement them technically. A brochure site stores no medical records.

Can we publish offers and prices on the site?

Technically yes, and we build an offers section managed from the dashboard. But advertising healthcare services is regulated, so your facility confirms with the regulator what may be published before it goes live. We implement what you approve and do not give a regulatory opinion.

Is a ready-made template enough for a clinic?

It is for a small clinic that wants to introduce itself and take appointment requests by WhatsApp or a simple form. Once you have many doctors and branches and need a searchable directory, direct booking and two languages, a custom site is clearer for patients and easier to run.

Can patients pay the consultation fee on the site?

Where needed, yes, by mada, Apple Pay and cards through a licensed payment gateway your facility contracts with. If the site is to issue invoices, they are built to comply with ZATCA e-invoicing requirements, or passed to your accounting system to issue.

Who owns the site, and what happens after launch?

The domain, hosting and code are in the facility’s name, and the delivery date is written into the contract and kept. After launch you get fast technical support, and reception and marketing receive a video guide to updating doctors, schedules and offers from the dashboard without coming back to us.
