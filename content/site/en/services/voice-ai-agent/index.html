Who is this for?

  • Clinics and medical centres that miss booking calls at peak times
  • Maintenance and home-service companies taking phone requests all day
  • Real estate agencies and car showrooms that want every caller from their ads answered
  • Restaurants and branches that take orders and reservations by phone
  • Companies that want automated follow-up or reminder calls instead of dialling manually

What you get

Natural voice, clear dialect

Works with your existing phone line

Outbound reminder and follow-up calls

Appointment reminders, post-service follow-ups or order confirmations, within the hours and rules you set.

The call nobody answered

For many businesses, phone calls are the highest-intent leads: whoever calls wants a service now. Yet a share of those calls is lost because staff are on another line, or the call came after hours, on a weekend or during prayer time. A caller who gets no answer rarely calls back; they call the next competitor in the search results.

A voice agent does not get sick, take leave or get busy, and it can answer several calls at once. It does not fully replace your reception team; it takes the repetitive requests and transfers the cases that need a person, freeing the team for the callers who really need them.

With ad campaigns in particular the value is obvious: an ad that sends callers to a number nobody answers burns budget. A voice agent makes sure every call from the ad is answered and logged, so you know exactly how many calls came in and how many became appointments.

When a voice agent is a good fit, and when it is not

It is a good fit when most calls are repetitive: booking an appointment, asking about prices or branches, checking an order, logging a maintenance request. It is a weaker fit when calls are complex and need negotiation or careful diagnosis; there we design it to gather the initial information and then transfer.

We usually start by analysing a sample of your calls or asking your reception team for the top ten reasons people call. If three or four reasons cover most calls, the return on a voice agent is clear and measurable from the first month.

  • Good fit: bookings, frequent questions, logging requests, reminders and follow-ups
  • With transfer: sensitive complaints, price negotiation, emergencies
  • Not on its own: consultations that need professional judgement

How it works, in plain terms

A call goes through three stages in fractions of a second: the caller’s speech is turned into text, the text is understood and an answer or action is chosen from your company’s information and systems, then the reply is turned into natural speech. The speed of that loop is what makes the conversation feel natural, so we pick components with low latency for Arabic, and let callers interrupt the agent just as they would a person.

The agent connects to your phone line through a telecom provider that supports programmable calls. It can run on a new number or receive calls forwarded from your existing one. Every call is saved as a transcript and summary in your system, and the same agent can also run in a window on your website or inside your app.

Designing the script with your team

A good voice agent introduces itself clearly, keeps it short, confirms key details such as date, time and name before saving, and makes transferring to a person easy. We write the script with your receptionists because they know how customers actually talk and what comes after the first question.

Then we test it on trial calls with different dialects, speaking speeds and noisy settings such as a car or the street, and adjust before launch. We usually go live on a subset of calls first, such as after-hours calls, then expand.

Before any of that, try it yourself: /en/try on our website has a live voice agent you can talk to from your browser. Ask it what your customers would ask, interrupt it, switch topics mid-sentence, and within two minutes you will know whether this experience suits your customers and what you would change in tone or speed.

What drives the cost

The cost has two parts: building the agent once, and running it monthly. The build depends on the number of flows, connected systems such as booking and CRM, and how much the voice and tone are customised. Monthly running cost depends on call minutes, because every minute passes through speech recognition, the model, voice generation and telephony.

That is why our quote includes a monthly cost estimate based on your actual call volume, not a generic figure. If your volume is very low, we may recommend a WhatsApp agent first because it is cheaper to run, then add voice later on the same knowledge base.

How to measure a voice agent’s success

Before launch we agree on clear numbers to track monthly: the share of calls answered compared with before, the share of calls that ended in a booking or logged request without staff involvement, the transfer rate to staff and its reasons, and average call length. These numbers appear on a simple dashboard alongside call transcripts and summaries.

If transfers spike for a particular reason, we know the agent needs extra information or a new flow. If a question keeps coming up without an answer, we add it. With this regular review the agent improves over time instead of staying as delivered.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a voice AI agent cost?

A voice AI agent with us starts from EGP 37,500. The price has a build part, set by the number of flows, connected systems and how it connects to your phone line, and a monthly running part set mainly by call minutes. We ask for an estimate of daily calls, their average length and the top reasons people call, then send a written quote within 24 hours that itemises both parts with a realistic monthly estimate.

Does the agent understand Saudi Arabic on calls?

Yes. It understands Saudi and Gulf Arabic, other common Arabic dialects and speech mixed with English. Before launch we test it on trial calls in different dialects and noisy settings, add the terms specific to your business plus the names of branches, districts and services so it recognises them accurately, and review the first calls after launch to improve understanding.

Can I try the voice agent?

Yes. /en/try on our website has a live voice agent you can talk to straight from your browser, no sign-up needed. Ask it questions, interrupt it and change the subject, and you will see its understanding and response speed before you decide. Once we agree, we build a version on your company’s information that you and your team test before it is connected to your number.

Do I need to change my company’s number?

Not necessarily. Calls can be forwarded from your current number to the agent always, only when unanswered, or only after hours, depending on what your telecom provider and PBX allow. We review your current setup in the first session and suggest the simplest approach that does not change what your customers are used to or the number printed in your ads.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

We recommend the agent introduce itself as an automated assistant at the start of the call, because transparency builds trust and reduces frustration when a caller asks for something beyond the agent’s scope. Callers can ask for a person at any time: during working hours they are transferred immediately, and outside them a callback request is logged with a summary.

Are calls recorded?

A transcript and summary of every call can be saved, and audio recording is optional depending on your policy. We add a notice for callers at the start of the call, store data with defined permissions in line with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), and agree the retention period with you. The data and accounts remain your company’s property.

What is the difference between a voice agent and a traditional IVR?

A traditional IVR makes you press numbers and navigate long menus, and often ends with waiting for an agent. A voice agent understands what you say in your own words, answers, and carries out actions such as booking or logging a request. The result is a shorter call and an experience closer to talking with a staff member who knows your company well.

Can the agent make outbound calls?

Yes, for example reminding patients of appointments, confirming orders before shipping or checking satisfaction after a service. We agree with you on allowed hours, daily volume and rules, and only call people who have an existing relationship with your company. The outcome of each call is logged, so the team sees who confirmed, who asked to reschedule and who did not answer.

How long does it take to build a voice agent?

An agent for a single flow such as booking or logging requests can launch in 4 to 5 weeks, including script writing, integration and testing. Adding several flows, CRM integration and outbound calls can extend that to 8 weeks. We share progress with you weekly through the client portal.

Can the voice agent book appointments in our current system?

Yes, if your booking system or calendar allows reading and writing through an API, which most modern systems and Google and Microsoft calendars do. The agent reads open slots during the call, confirms the appointment and sends a written confirmation. If you do not have a suitable booking system, we can build a simple one connected to the agent.

Does the agent speak English for non-Arabic-speaking customers?

Yes. The agent detects the caller’s language and replies in it, serving Arabic and non-Arabic speakers on the same number. Other languages common among your customers can be added as needed, with each language tested before it goes live, because understanding and voice quality vary from one language to another.
