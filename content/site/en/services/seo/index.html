Why is your website not showing on Google?

On most sites we review, the problem is not a lack of articles but the foundation: pages built entirely in the visitor's browser so search engines cannot see their content, a site that is slow on mobile, one services page trying to rank for twenty keywords, or the same title and description on every page. Publishing more does not fix these problems; technical fixes come first.

Because we are a software company before we are a marketing provider, we fix these problems in the code itself, not just with plugins: moving important pages to server-side rendering, tuning performance and building a logical internal linking structure. That is the difference between an SEO service that makes recommendations in a report and one that implements them.

  • Pages that cannot be read without running JavaScript.
  • Slow mobile performance and uncompressed images.
  • One page for all services instead of one page per service.
  • Two domains live at once, or broken redirects after a redesign.

Service and city SEO in Egypt

Your customers usually search in a "service + city" format: maintenance company in Egypt, lawyer in Egypt, website design in Egypt. Each of those searches has different competitors and clear buying intent. So we build a page map linking each service to the cities you actually serve, with content specific to each city's sectors and needs, not a template where only the city name changes.

Google now penalises low-value duplicate pages. A good city page answers real questions: how the service is delivered there, the usual timeline, and what is different, with FAQs of its own.

Visibility in AI answers (AEO)

A growing share of buyers ask ChatGPT or Gemini, or read Google AI Overviews, before opening any website. These systems cite pages that answer clearly and directly, whose text is easy to read, and that carry consistent structured data.

So we write every important page answer-first: an opening paragraph that answers the question with figures and timelines, then the details, then FAQs. We add an llms.txt file and allow AI crawlers to read the site, while keeping private pages protected.

How to choose an SEO company and compare quotes

Be wary of anyone who guarantees you the top spot on Google within days; nobody can make that guarantee. And be wary of quotes that measure success by the number of articles or links alone. Ask instead: which keywords are you targeting, and why? Which technical fixes will you implement yourselves? And how will you measure enquiries from search, not just traffic?

Make sure your Google Search Console, Google Analytics and Business Profile accounts are in your company's name, and that the content written for you is yours if the service stops. That is how we work: every account and every piece of content is yours, and the contract is monthly with a clear scope.

  • No guaranteed ranking: ask for a plan and measurement, not promises.
  • Ask for examples of technical fixes, not just reports.
  • Make sure the accounts and content are in your name.

How we work in the first three months

Month one is for the audit, technical fixes, keyword research and connecting analytics, including tracking WhatsApp and call clicks as conversions. Month two is for rebuilding or creating the core service pages and optimising your Business Profile. Month three starts regular content, city pages and price guides.

At the end of every month you get a short report: what was done, what changed in rankings, traffic and enquiries, and next month's plan. And if your site needs a rebuild rather than a patch, we tell you so plainly and give you both options.

Common mistakes that hurt Saudi websites' rankings

What we see most on Arabic websites: copied city pages where only the city name changes, auto-generated articles with no review that answer no real question, and links bought from sites unrelated to the industry. These tactics can lift the numbers for a few weeks, then end in a drop that is hard to recover from after Google updates.

Another, less obvious mistake: redesigning a site or moving it to a new platform without a plan to redirect old URLs, so pages that brought in traffic for years disappear. If you are planning a redesign, bring us in before launch, not after, so we can inventory the important pages and preserve their value.

  • Duplicate city pages with the same content.
  • Automated content without expert human review.
  • Bought links from irrelevant sites.
  • A redesign without redirects for old URLs.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SEO cost in Egypt?

In the Saudi market, monthly SEO plans start at around EGP 15,000 for small sites, common plans for companies run EGP 30,000 to 75,000 per month, and more for large stores and high competition. The price depends on the state of the site, the number of pages and cities, and keyword competition. We send a written quote within 24 hours. It specifies the fixes, pages and monthly content in numbers, so you compare quotes on the work delivered, not the plan name.

When will I see SEO results?

Technical fixes can improve indexing within weeks, but ranking for competitive keywords usually takes 3 to 6 months, and longer in high-competition fields. SEO is a compounding investment: a good page keeps bringing in customers for months after it is written. So we start with keywords closest to purchase and with lower competition to get early wins, then move on to the harder ones.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and nobody can honestly guarantee it, because Google does not sell organic rankings. What we commit to in writing is the monthly scope of work: fixes, pages, content and reports. And we measure success by enquiries from search, not by rankings alone. Anyone who guarantees you first place within days is usually relying on keywords nobody searches for, or tactics that put your site at risk of a penalty.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

Google Ads brings instant traffic that stops when you stop paying, while SEO builds free, lasting visibility but takes time. The best approach is usually to combine them: ads for urgent keywords at the start, then scale them down as organic visibility grows. Ad data also helps SEO, because it shows which keywords bring real enquiries before you invest in content for them.

Do you work on WordPress, Salla and Zid websites?

Yes, though what can be done differs. On WordPress and custom-built sites we control everything technically. On Salla and Zid we work within what the platform allows: titles, descriptions, category structure, content and speed where possible, and we tell you each platform's limits up front. If the platform's limits block your search growth, we suggest solutions such as a blog or landing pages connected to the store.

Do you write the content in Arabic or translate it?

We write in clear Modern Standard Arabic suited to Gulf readers, starting from your customers' real questions and what they search for, not by translating English content. A separate English version can be produced for bilingual sites, with the two languages linked correctly. Someone on your side who knows the field reviews the content before publishing, because accuracy in the details is what separates useful content from generic content.

Do I need to redesign my website to improve SEO?

Not always. Many sites improve with technical fixes and new pages. But if search engines cannot read the site, or it is slow in a way that cannot be fixed, a rebuild is cheaper in the long run. We tell you which applies after the initial audit. If you decide to rebuild, we preserve old URLs with proper redirects so you do not lose the rankings your site has built.

Does SEO include showing up on Google Maps?

Yes. We optimise your Google Business Profile: categories, services, description, photos and opening hours, and help you set up a system for requesting reviews from your real customers. This matters a lot for local searches such as "near me". We also make sure your name, address and phone number match across the website, the profile and directories, because inconsistencies weaken local visibility.

How do I know SEO is actually bringing in customers?

We set up analytics so contact forms, WhatsApp clicks and calls from organic search are recorded as conversions. The monthly report shows these numbers alongside rankings and traffic, so you see the return, not just indicators. That way you know which pages and keywords actually bring in customers, and we focus on them in the following months instead of spreading effort evenly.

Do I keep the accounts and content if I stop the service?

Yes. Your Search Console, Analytics and Business Profile accounts are in your company's name and we work on them with added access, and all the content and pages we produce stay on your site and remain yours after the contract ends. The contract is monthly with a clear scope, so you are not locked into a long term before you see how we work and the results of the first months.
