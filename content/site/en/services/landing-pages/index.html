Why not send your ads to the homepage?

Your homepage speaks to everyone: every service, the company story, news and careers. A visitor coming from an ad about one specific service is looking for one thing, and if they do not find it on the first screen they leave, and you have paid for the click with nothing to show for it.

A landing page strips out every distraction: no top menu with ten links, nothing outside the offer. A headline that repeats the ad's promise, then the benefit, then the proof, then a single button. That is why landing pages usually convert better than general pages, and why they make ad reports clearer: every campaign has its own page and its own metric.

There is a less obvious benefit too. Ad platforms rate the quality of the page you send visitors to. A fast page whose content matches the ad text usually earns a better score, which affects your cost per click and ad position. A good page does not just lift conversions, it can lower what you pay for each visit.

The anatomy of a landing page that sells

We do not start with design, we start with the message. Who is the visitor? What worries them? What makes them ask now rather than later? Then we order the sections so the visitor finds the answer before they ask the question.

We write in language close to your audience while keeping it clear, and we avoid empty claims like "the best" or "number one" that nobody believes. Instead we use details that can be checked: what the customer gets, when, and on what terms.

  • A headline that matches the ad copy, plus one line on the benefit
  • The call-to-action button visible on a phone without scrolling
  • The customer's problem in their own words
  • The offer in detail: what they get, when, and the price if you want to show it
  • Trust signals: real photos, guarantees and business details
  • FAQs that answer the objections you expect
  • The call-to-action button repeated at the bottom of the page

Tracking: the part most people skip

A landing page without proper tracking leaves the ad platform working blind. When Google or Meta cannot tell which click became a real lead, they keep sending cheap traffic that never buys, and you conclude that ads do not work in your industry.

We set up every form submission and every WhatsApp or call click as a conversion event, and pass the campaign source with the lead to your sales team or CRM. Later you can see which campaign brought a paying customer, not just which one brought a form. We also add a privacy policy and a cookie consent prompt in line with the Personal Data Protection Law.

If you have a CRM, we send the lead straight into it with the campaign name and keyword, so your reports show which ad produced a closed deal and for how much. That connection moves the conversation with your marketing team from "how many leads did we get?" to "how many riyals did we earn for every riyal we spent?"

Common mistakes that burn ad budgets

We review a lot of landing pages for live campaigns, and the same mistakes keep coming up. Most of them do not need a bigger budget, just clearer decisions on the page itself. Fixing one of them can change your cost per lead more than any targeting tweak.

So for any live campaign we start by reviewing the current page and its numbers: bounce rate, time until content appears on mobile, and how many people started the form but did not finish. Then we write down what we will change and why, before we design anything.

  • A long form asking for details sales does not need on first contact
  • A heavy page with uncompressed video or images that loads slowly on mobile
  • A generic headline unrelated to the ad the visitor clicked
  • More than one offer or more than one button on the same page
  • No WhatsApp number, or a number nobody answers after hours
  • No tracking, so the campaign is judged by clicks instead of customers

One page or several?

If you advertise more than one service, or target different segments such as consumers and businesses, one page per segment beats a single page trying to please everyone. The same goes for cities: one page for Riyadh clients and another for Jeddah if the offer, lead time or sales team differs.

To keep costs down, we build one master template in your brand and produce a version for each campaign by swapping only the copy and images. That way you launch a new campaign in days instead of weeks, and test different offers without redesigning every time.

For seasons such as Ramadan, Saudi National Day and back to school, we simply prepare a seasonal version of the same template with a different offer and copy, then restore the main page when the season ends, without losing your tracking setup or past campaign data.

Before the campaign goes live: what we need from you

We need the offer details and terms, real photos of the product or service, the current ad if there is one, and the WhatsApp number that will receive the leads. We also need access to your ad accounts to add tracking tags, or we work directly with your marketing lead or the agency running your campaigns.

Before launch we test the page on different phones and send a test lead to make sure it reaches your team and registers as a conversion on the ad platform. Then the campaign starts and you know every riyal is being measured.

Two weeks into the campaign we review the first numbers with you: where visitors come from, where they stop on the page, and how many sent a request. Based on that we propose a specific change to the headline, the form or the section order instead of guessing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a landing page cost?

A landing page with us starts from EGP 84,000; the final price depends on the design, copywriting, tracking setup, the number of test variants and CRM integration. We send a written quote within 24 hours once we understand the campaign and the offer.

How long does a landing page take?

Usually one to two weeks, including copywriting, design, development, tracking setup and testing. If the content and images are ready and you already have a template with us, a new campaign version can go live within a few days. The delivery date is written into the contract so you can plan the campaign launch around it.

What is the difference between a landing page and a website?

A website introduces the whole company and serves visitors with many different goals, while a landing page serves one campaign and one goal. Neither replaces the other: the website builds trust and search visibility, and the landing page turns ad traffic into leads. The landing page can live on your own website.

Do you run the ads as well?

Our focus is building the page and setting up tracking correctly, because that is what makes any campaign measurable. We work with your marketing team or the agency running your ads, hand them conversion events that are ready and tested, and adjust the page based on campaign results.

Do you guarantee a certain number of leads?

No, and no serious provider should promise that, because results also depend on the offer, budget, targeting and ad quality. What we commit to is a fast page built on best practice, accurate tracking that shows where customers drop off, and test variants to improve the result.

Can leads go straight to WhatsApp?

Yes. The button can open a WhatsApp chat with a pre-filled message about the offer, or the form can be sent to your sales number the moment it is submitted. It can also connect to an AI WhatsApp assistant that answers common questions and books appointments after hours, so leads do not go cold before your team replies.

Can I edit the page myself?

Yes. We build the page so you can change text, prices, dates and images from a simple dashboard and prepare the next campaign without waiting, and we give you a short video showing how. Adding new sections or changing the design is estimated in a small, clear quote before any work starts.

Will the landing page show up in Google search results?

Ad campaign pages are usually blocked from search engines so they do not compete with or duplicate your website pages. If you want a page that attracts free search traffic, that is a service page with a different structure and content, and we build it as part of our SEO service.

Will the page be on my company domain?

Yes, on your domain or a subdomain of it, with the accounts and code in your name. We do not use platforms that keep you subscribed to us, so if we stop working together the pages, tracking data and analytics accounts remain fully yours and any other provider can carry on the work.

How do payments and follow-up work?

Payments are staged: part on signing and the rest once you see the page working on a staging link and approve it. We work on Egypt time, follow up through a WhatsApp group and a short online meeting when needed, and provide fast technical support after launch to fix any bug quickly.
