Who is this for?

  • Companies whose number lives in the WhatsApp Business app on one employee’s phone, where the manager cannot see the conversations
  • Sales and support teams that need several people working on the same number
  • Companies that want order confirmations, reminders and invoices sent automatically from their systems
  • Organisations that tried unofficial tools and had their number restricted or banned
  • Companies planning a digital employee on WhatsApp that want the right foundation first

What you get

An official number in your company’s name

We set the number up on WhatsApp’s official business interface. The number and the Meta business account stay in your company’s name, not ours and not a provider’s.

Account verification and display name

We guide you through business verification with Meta and approval of the display name, and prepare the documents required. The final decision on approval is Meta’s.

Message templates

We write the confirmation, reminder, follow-up and offer templates in line with Meta’s rules, submit them for approval, and rework any that are rejected.

A shared inbox

One conversation panel for the team: assigning a chat to a person, internal notes, the status of each conversation, and a manager’s view of everything going on.

A link to your CRM and systems

We connect the number to your customer, order or booking system where the system offers an interface, so the conversation appears with the customer’s record and every new contact is logged.

Automatic notifications from your systems

An order confirmation, a shipping update, a reminder of an appointment or an instalment: messages that leave your system automatically as approved templates, to people who agreed to receive them.

Broadcasts within the rules

Campaigns to lists whose owners agreed to hear from you, as approved templates, at a volume that rises gradually to protect the number’s quality, with a clear way to opt out.

Ready for the digital employee

The same setup is the foundation a digital employee later runs on: it replies automatically and hands over to staff in the shared inbox with a summary.

The WhatsApp Business app or the official API?

The WhatsApp Business app suits a small business where one or two people reply from one phone. It gets cramped quickly: the conversations sit on an employee’s device, the manager cannot tell who answered whom, nothing connects to the customer system, and when the employee leaves, the chat history leaves with them.

The official API is not an app you open. It is a channel that software connects to: a shared inbox, a customer system or a digital employee. On it, several people work on the same number, notifications leave your systems automatically, and conversations are kept by the company, not by individuals. In return it has clearer rules and a cost on some kinds of message.

  • The app: one device or a few, manual replies, no link to your systems
  • The official interface: a whole team on one number, links to your systems, automatic notifications, approved templates
  • Unofficial tools that automate the ordinary app: outside WhatsApp’s terms, and they put the number at risk of a ban

The setup steps, and what we need from you

The setup itself is not complicated, but some steps sit with Meta, take their own time and are in nobody else’s control. So we start those on day one and build the integration alongside them.

  • A Meta business account in the company’s name, with the business details and documents verification requires
  • The number: a new dedicated number, or a move of the current one, with a clear account of what the move involves
  • The display name customers will see, with the profile picture and details
  • A list of the messages you want to send, so we can write them as templates and submit them
  • The systems the number will connect to, and who in the team will work in the shared inbox
  • A pilot with a small group of customers before the number opens to everyone

Templates and the reply window: the two rules behind everything

When a customer writes to you, a time window opens in which you can reply freely, with ordinary text typed by a person or generated by a digital employee. Once the window closes, or when you want to start the conversation yourself, only a template Meta approved in advance can be sent.

That changes how you think about messages. Every notification, reminder or offer has to be written as a template with clear variables such as the name, the order number and the appointment time, and placed in its correct category. We write the templates with you, explain why some are occasionally rejected and how to reword them, and test each one on the team’s own numbers before it reaches a customer.

A shared inbox and a link to the customer system

After the move to the official interface, the team works from a shared conversation panel instead of a phone. Each conversation has an owner and a status, the manager sees what has not been answered, and internal notes stay with the chat. The panel can be a ready-made platform on subscription or part of a system we build for you, and we set out the difference in cost and flexibility before you choose.

The link to the customer system is what makes the number part of the business and not an island: a new contact is recorded automatically, the conversation appears in their record, and automatic messages go out when an order changes status or an appointment approaches. We connect where the system offers an interface, and tell you plainly where it does not.

Broadcasts within Meta’s rules

Broadcasts on the official interface are allowed on conditions: an approved template, a list whose owners agreed to hear from you, and an easy way to opt out. Each number starts with a sending limit that rises gradually as long as it keeps good quality, and falls if many customers block or report it.

So we build sending in stages: a small segment first, then we raise the volume and watch quality. We connect the lists to the customer system so the right message reaches the right segment. We do not send to bought lists or to anyone who has not agreed, because that is the shortest route to a restricted number and the worst thing for a brand’s name.

What nobody can guarantee, and who owns what

Business verification, approval of the display name and of templates, and the verified badge are decisions Meta alone makes. We prepare the application as well as it can be prepared and follow it up, but anyone who promises a guaranteed result or a fixed timescale is promising something they do not control. Nor does the integration itself reply to customers: either your team replies from the shared inbox, or you add a digital employee, which is tested on your customers’ real messages before it is switched on.

In our projects the number, the Meta business account, the templates and the conversations belong to your company, and you can move them to another provider. Conversations are kept with defined access in line with the requirements of the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law. Meta’s message fees are paid to Meta by message type, and we show them in the quote as a line separate from our own fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much does WhatsApp Business API integration cost?

Ready-made WhatsApp platforms start in the market at subscriptions of about EGP 480 to 8,235 a month, which is enough if you need a shared inbox and campaigns. A custom integration with your systems, such as a CRM, booking or orders, starts with us from EGP 37,500, with the final price set by the number of systems and flows. Meta’s message fees are paid to Meta. We send a written quote within 24 hours.

Can I use my current number?

Usually yes, but a number running on the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app is moved to the official interface, after which it is used from the shared inbox and not from the app in the same way. If the team relies on that number day to day as it is, we sometimes suggest a dedicated number. We explain both options and what each involves before we start.

How long does Meta’s approval take?

It varies from case to case and can take from days to weeks, depending on how complete the business documents are and on the type of request. No provider controls it, and we promise no fixed timescale. What we do is start verification on day one and build the integration alongside it, so launch is delayed no longer than it must be.

What does Meta charge for messages?

Meta charges for messages the business starts, by type. The figures published by service providers for Saudi Arabia are about EGP 2.85 for a marketing message and about EGP 0.6 for a utility message or verification code. They can change, so check them when you sign. A reply inside a conversation the customer started is treated differently.

Can the number be banned on the official interface?

The risk is far lower than with unofficial tools, but it exists if the rules are broken: sending to people who did not agree, content against policy, or many customers blocking the number. We protect the number by raising volume gradually, using consented lists, offering a clear opt-out and watching the quality rating.

Will I get the green badge?

The verified badge is granted by Meta under its own criteria and is not a guaranteed part of any setup. We can prepare and submit the request if your company qualifies, but we do not promise it. Your company’s name appears to customers once the display name is approved, even without the badge.

Will you broadcast to a list of numbers I have?

Only if their owners agreed to receive messages from your company, and only as a template Meta has approved. We do not send to bought lists or lists gathered without consent. We start with a small segment, raise the volume gradually and include an easy way to opt out in every message.

Does it connect to the customer system we use?

Where the system offers an interface we connect directly, so conversations appear with the customer’s record and notifications go out automatically. Where it does not, we use an automation tool in between or a sheet, or build a customer system that fits you. We review your system first and write down what can and cannot be done.

Who owns the number and the account after setup?

Your company. The number, the Meta business account, the templates and the conversations stay in your name and under your control, and we work on them with access you grant and can withdraw. That is written into the contract, and it means you can change platform or provider later without losing the number.
