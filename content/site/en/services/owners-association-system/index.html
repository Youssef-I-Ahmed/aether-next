Owners Association and Residential Compound Management System

Get a quote on WhatsAppRequest a quote

An owners association management system organises the money and shared services of a building or compound: a register of units, owners and tenants, shared service charges allocated by area or share, collection and WhatsApp reminders, maintenance requests and work orders, a transparent budget and expenses, and meetings and voting. It comes with a portal where each owner sees what they owe and their requests. We build it for owners associations, facility management companies and developers handing over compounds. Implementation takes 8 to 14 weeks, and custom systems with us start from EGP 282,000. You get a written quote within 24 hours.

Who is this for?

  • Owners associations in residential and commercial towers and buildings
  • Facility management companies running several associations and compounds
  • Real estate developers handing over compounds who want after-sale management
  • Gated compounds with shared services, gates and security

What you get

Unit and owner register

Every unit with its area, share, owner and tenant if any, with a history of ownership transfers.

Service charges and allocation

An annual budget for shared services allocated to units by area or share, with periodic demands issued to each owner.

Collection and reminders

Payment links with mada and Apple Pay, automatic WhatsApp reminders, and a statement for each owner showing paid and overdue amounts.

Expenses and transparency

Every expense with its invoice, supplier and budget line, and a regular report showing owners where their money went.

Maintenance requests

An owner or resident raises a request with a photo, it is assigned to a technician or contractor, and its status is tracked to closure.

Contracts and suppliers

Security, cleaning, lift and maintenance contracts with renewal dates and amounts due, and an alert before expiry.

Meetings and voting

General assembly invitations, the agenda, electronic voting weighted by share, and minutes stored in the system.

Owner portal

Each owner sees their dues, requests, reports and announcements on their phone without calling management.

Why owners association fees go unpaid

Owners pay easily when they understand what they are paying for and trust it is spent where it should be. They pay late when they receive a demand with no breakdown, or hear that maintenance is behind and don’t know why.

The system fixes trust before collection: a clear budget, expenses with their invoices, and a regular report every owner sees in their portal. Then come online collection and automatic reminders, and overdue amounts fall without chasing.

The system and the official platform

Owners associations in the Kingdom are registered and regulated through the competent authority and its official platform, and our system does not replace that. It is the day-to-day operating tool for the association or the facility management company: accounts, services, requests and communication.

We record the association’s official details and reference numbers in the system, and we don’t promise an automatic link with any official platform unless it offers one on its own terms.

What owners see in the portal

The portal cuts calls and messages to management and gives owners a sense of control over what they pay:

  • A statement of dues and payments with payment links
  • Their requests, status and a photo of the completed work
  • The budget, regular reports and expenses
  • Announcements, meeting invitations and voting
  • Association documents and assembly resolutions

For facility management companies

A company managing ten compounds needs to see each compound separately, with its own accounts, requests and technicians, and the whole picture at the same time. We build the system so each association has its own space and permissions, with a company dashboard that brings them together.

It can be linked to an app for field technicians, and to invoices for management fees that meet ZATCA’s e-invoicing requirements.

Ready-made or custom?

A small association in a single building may be fine with cloud accounting software, a unit spreadsheet and a group chat. Don’t build a system before you need one.

A custom system suits large compounds, mixed-use towers and facility management companies: charge allocation with its own rules, voting by share, an owner portal, and many supplier contracts. You own the code and the data, with no fee per unit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an owners association management system cost?

Cloud accounting subscriptions run at EGP 285–14,985 a month but don’t manage units or requests, and a custom system costs EGP 282,000–562,500 in the market. Custom systems with us start from EGP 282,000, and we send the quote in writing within 24 hours.

Does it allocate service charges to units automatically?

Yes. You set the annual budget for shared services, the system allocates it to units by area, share or a rule you define, and it issues periodic demands to each owner.

Does it support electronic voting at meetings?

Yes. The invitation and agenda are sent to owners, each owner votes from their portal according to their share, and the minutes and result are stored in the system.

Does it replace registration on the official platform?

No. Official registration and regulation of the association go through the competent authority. Our system is the day-to-day tool for accounts, services and communication, and it records the official details and reference numbers.

Does it suit a company managing several compounds?

Yes. Each compound has its own accounts, requests and permissions, the company has a dashboard covering all of them, and it comes with a technician app and invoicing for management fees.

Do we own the code and the data?

Yes. The source code and the database are in the association’s or company’s name, and the contract says so. The delivery date is written down, payments are tied to stages, and after launch you get fast technical support.
